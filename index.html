Propane + water: flash, then stripping

A propane + water feed enters a flash vessel. The vapour leaves the top; the liquid leaves the bottom and becomes the feed to a stripping column, where inert gas in at the bottom removes the remaining propane. Change any input in the sidebar and every number below updates.

Column flows use the model's constant-molar-overflow assumption: liquid and gas molar flows are held constant through the stripper (the standard Kremser assumption). Gas-stream compositions are propane only — the rest of the gas is inert, and water transfer to the gas is neglected in this model.
